The Project

An operating gold site in the heart of Bétaré-Oya

The concession of LANKEU PATRICE LEMON covers four hectares in the East Region of the Republic of Cameroon, within the renowned Bétaré-Oya gold district — an area with extensive gold-bearing alluvial gravels of proven economic importance, roughly 160 km from Bertoua, the regional capital.

Artisanal and semi-mechanized gold production is already active on site: excavators, prospecting pits and sluice washing. Official expertise (MINMIDT, Yaoundé) certified gold bars of 22–22.5 carat (≈ 91.7–93.75% purity) produced by the operator.

Remote-sensing survey (RSS) outlined three gold ore zones (Au-1, Au-2, Au-3) and one silver-bearing zone (Ag-1) as priority exploration targets. The East Region is also known for its alluvial diamond occurrences, making the area a combined gold & diamond prospect.
