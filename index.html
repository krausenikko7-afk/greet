The Project

An operating gold site in the heart of Bétaré-Oya

The concession of LANKEU PATRICE LEMON covers four hectares in the East Region of the Republic of Cameroon, within the renowned Bétaré-Oya gold district — an area with extensive gold-bearing alluvial gravels of proven economic importance, roughly 160 km from Bertoua, the regional capital.

Artisanal and semi-mechanized gold production is already active on site: excavators, prospecting pits and sluice washing. Official expertise (MINMIDT, Yaoundé) certified gold bars of 22–22.5 carat (≈ 91.7–93.75% purity) produced by the operator.

Remote-sensing survey (RSS) outlined three gold ore zones (Au-1, Au-2, Au-3) and one silver-bearing zone (Ag-1) as priority exploration targets. The East Region is also known for its alluvial diamond occurrences, making the area a combined gold & diamond prospect.

How We Work

From alluvial gravel to certified gold bars

1

Targeting

Remote-sensing survey outlines the ore zones (Au-1…Au-3, Ag-1) and guides pit placement.

2

Excavation

Excavator opens the gold-bearing gravel layers; prospecting pits verify the pay streak.

3

Sluice Washing

Gravel is washed over sluice lines; heavy gold concentrate settles and is panned clean.

4

Smelting

Concentrate is smelted on site into raw gold bars ready for assay.

5

Certification

MINMIDT (Yaoundé) assays each batch — certified 22–22.5 carat purity.

Republic of Cameroon

Cameroon — “Africa in miniature”

Cameroon stretches from Atlantic beaches and an active volcano to rainforest and Sahel — a bilingual (French & English) republic of about 28 million people, member of CEMAC with the stable CFA franc pegged to the euro.

The East Region is its treasure chest: alluvial gold of Bétaré-Oya and Batouri, diamond occurrences near Kette and Mobilong, timber and wild landscapes along the Lom and Djérem rivers.
